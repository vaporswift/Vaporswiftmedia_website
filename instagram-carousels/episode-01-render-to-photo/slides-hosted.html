01
Why it works

Seven slots.
Same order, every time.

PRESERVE
The one everybody skips. Without it the model redesigns the building you were hired to sell.
CAMERA
Body, lens, height, exposure. "Verticals corrected" is the actual signature of architectural photography — AI defaults to converging lines, which reads as amateur.
LIGHT
Time of day, direction, colour temperature. Warm interiors against a cold sky is the twilight-shot formula.
MATERIAL
Name two or three real finishes. "Wood" gives you plastic. "White oak with grain variation" gives you wood.
ATMOSPHERE
Wet ground buys you reflections. Reflections are physics, and physics is what your eye reads as real.
MEDIUM
Name the publication, not the software. Grain and vignette are the fingerprints of a real sensor.
NOT
Name the failure modes out loud. The word "perfect" is what ruins most AI architecture.
