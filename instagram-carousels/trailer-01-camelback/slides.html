Why it feels shot, not generated

Five locked frames.
One move.

"The camera does
not follow it"
Real films are shot on tripods and rigs. So we banned camera movement in five of six shots — and spent the only move on the last one, rushing his back through the door.

Stillness is what makes the one move land.
