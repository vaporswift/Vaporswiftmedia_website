Why it works

Stop describing
the camera.
Show it.

THE PROBLEM
Text is a terrible way to specify motion. "Slow push-in" has a hundred valid readings and the model picks one at random. So you re-roll, and re-roll.
THE FIX
Pin a start frame and an end frame. Now the model only has to invent the path between two fixed points.
WHAT CHANGES
Composition is locked at both ends, so the prompt shrinks to describing motion only. Five lines instead of a paragraph — and far more reliable.
WHERE THEY COME FROM
The start frame is the twilight shot from Episode 01. Your stills library is the raw material for video.
THE PATTERN
Same idea as PRESERVE in Ep 01 and the clay pass: give the model constraints as images, not adjectives.

Six words save
the whole shot.

Leave it out and the model treats the doorway as a cut point. You get two shots stitched together instead of one continuous move — and the entire effect dies, because the effect was the continuity.
