# Episode 03 — Caption

Carousel leads with the video (slide 1), 7 teaching slides behind it.

Instagram truncates around 125 characters, so the hook has to land before the
"more" cut. Everything after it is for people who already stopped.

---

A drone flies at this house, through the front door, and into the living room without a single cut — and I never described the camera move.

Here's how.

You give it two pictures instead of a paragraph.

start_image — the twilight exterior.
end_image — the lit interior.

Now the model isn't inventing a shot. It's solving for the path between two frames you already chose. Composition is locked at both ends, so the prompt only has to describe motion.

That's the whole prompt, and it's five lines:

One continuous flying shot, no cuts. The camera glides forward toward the house, rises slightly, and passes through the open front door without stopping — carrying straight on into the warm lit interior and settling on the living space.

Smooth FPV drone motion, constant forward momentum, never pausing at the threshold. Cool twilight light gives way to warm interior light as we cross inside. Quiet evening ambience.

No people. No text.

Six words are carrying the entire shot: "never pausing at the threshold."

Leave them out and the model reads the doorway as a cut point. You get two shots stitched together instead of one continuous move, and the effect dies — because the effect WAS the continuity.

Why this beats writing a better camera description: "slow push-in" has a hundred valid readings and the model picks one at random. So you re-roll, and re-roll, and never land the shot you saw in your head. Two frames end the guessing.

One more thing. The start frame is the twilight exterior from Episode 01. Nothing new was shot for this. Every still you make is now two assets.

Save this for the next place you've already photographed.

Send it to someone planning a shoot the budget won't quite cover.

Episode 04: daylight → golden hour, same geometry. Follow so it lands.
