What you're actually grading

Four tells in
any answer.

The tell
A
B
Did it do the whole instruction?
Or the easy half. You asked for two things — count them.
Did it tell you what it changed?
A new version with no account of the edits can't be checked.
Did it invent anything?
A number, a date, a claim you never gave it. This is the one that costs you.
Did it fold when you pushed?
"That's not right" should get a question back — not an instant apology and a worse answer.
Whichever column has more marks is your answer. It is allowed to disagree with the internet.
