Claude or ChatGPT? Every comparison you've read expires in about a month. Here's a test that doesn't.

Here's how to run it.

Both of them ship new models constantly. Any post ranking them — including the one you read last week — was written against versions that have already been replaced. Meanwhile the thing you actually need them for hasn't changed at all.

So stop reading verdicts and run twenty minutes of your own work through both.

Open them side by side. Same inputs, same order, same day. Don't help one of them more than the other.

1. YOUR REAL WORK
Give it something you actually made — a caption, a client email, a scope of work.
"Rewrite this. Keep every fact. Then list what you changed and why."

2. PUSH BACK
Reply to whichever answer you liked less with three words: "That's not right."
Say nothing else, and watch what it does.

3. YOUR OWN FILE
Upload a document you know cold. Ask something only someone who read all of it could answer.

Then grade both answers on four things:

Did it do the whole instruction, or the easy half? You asked for two things. Count them.

Did it tell you what it changed? A new version handed back with no account of the edits can't be checked, which means you have to re-read the whole thing yourself.

Did it invent anything? A number, a date, a claim you never gave it. This is the one that costs you money, because it's the one you'll paste into something real.

Did it fold when you pushed? "That's not right" should get a question back — what specifically, or here's what I based that on. An instant apology followed by a worse answer is a tool that will agree with you all the way off a cliff.

Prompt 2 is the one most people skip and the one that tells you the most. It's the difference between something that helps you think and something that just agrees with you fast.

Whichever column wins is your answer. It's allowed to disagree with the internet, because the internet isn't doing your job.

And "both, for different things" is a completely legitimate result. Most people who use these seriously end up there.

One note on what's deliberately not in these slides: no prices, no model names, no feature checklists. All three change faster than a post like this ages, and a confidently wrong number on a teaching post is worse than no number. Check the source when you need the current one.

Save this and re-run it the next time either one ships something big.
