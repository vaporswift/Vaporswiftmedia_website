## Benchmarks

Ignore likes — they're the weakest of the three signals Instagram weights.
Track these four:

| Metric | Why | Starting target |
|---|---|---|
| **Saves ÷ reach** | What a carousel is for | >3% |
| **Sends ÷ reach** | Heaviest distribution signal right now | >1% |
| **Swipe-through to last slide** | Whether the teaching holds | >40% |
| **Follows ÷ reach** | The only number that says the brand-build is working | >1% |

These are starting lines, not truth. After four posts, replace them with your
own median — that's the real benchmark.

## Open questions these posts should answer

Written down before posting so they can't be rationalised afterwards.

1. **What kind of work do people actually ask about?** Read the replies and
   DMs, not the demographics. Whatever people bring their own problem about is
   what episode 06 should be, and which VaporSwift service is worth surfacing.
   This is not about choosing a persona to write for — the account speaks to
   anyone who could save time or money with these tools.
2. **Does video-led beat still-led?** A03 leads with video, A01/A02 lead with a
   still. Compare swipe-through — if video-led wins, convert the others.
3. **Does the 3:4 reframe cost too much?** A03's drone move was shot 9:16 and
   cropped to 3:4. If it underperforms B01 (native 9:16 Reel), the crop is
   eating the vertical drama and video should stay a standalone Reel.
4. **Which track earns follows?** Real estate converts to clients; Track B
   probably reaches further. Both matter, but only one should get the bulk of
   the effort.
5. **Do the two-part prompt slides lose people?** A01 and A02 split the prompt
   across slides 3 and 4. If swipe-through falls off a cliff at slide 4,
   condense instead.
6. **Does the tool audience overlap the work audience?** Track A/B sell what
   the tools can make; C02 and C03 explain the tool itself and will pull a
   different, broader crowd. Watch follows-per-reach against saves-per-reach:
   if C posts follow well but save poorly, they're reach, not depth, and
   belong at a lower frequency than A and B.
7. **Do drawn diagrams save as well as photographs?** C02 is the first deck
   with no photography in it at all. If its saves-per-reach holds against A01
   and A02, the diagram master is cheap content that doesn't need a
   generation budget — which changes what's worth building next.
8. **Does a post that refuses to pick a winner still perform?** C03 hands over
   a test instead of a verdict, which is the honest version and the less
   clickable one. If it underperforms badly on sends, the format needs a
   sharper hook — not a fake verdict.

## Lessons

Append as you go — dated, one per entry. Include the misses; a format that
flopped is worth more than one that did fine for reasons you can't name.

### Pre-launch (build phase)

- **Instagram's 3:4 support is real but under-documented.** Most sizing guides
  still say 4:5 is the ceiling; that's pre-2025. 3:4 renders uncropped in feed
  *and* grid. Some third-party schedulers still enforce the old 1350 cap and
  silently downscale — post natively.
- **Carousels get a second serve.** Instagram re-surfaces them using a later
  slide as the cover, so slide 2 has to hook cold. Designing slide 2 as a
  throwaway wastes half the distribution.
- **The copyable prompt belongs in the caption, not just the image.** Image
  text isn't selectable. This is the single biggest save driver in the format.
- **Ask for the send explicitly.** Sends-per-reach is a top-three signal and
  almost nobody asks for it directly.
