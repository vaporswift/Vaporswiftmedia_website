# Free Fonts & Vetted Asset Sources

The "premium fonts" in paid packs are almost always free fonts you can get yourself. Here's
the mapping plus where to safely get everything.

---

## Free fonts that match the "premium" pack look

Install via Google Fonts (free, commercial use) or the noted source. After installing, restart
Premiere so they appear.

| You want this look | Free font | Source |
|--------------------|-----------|--------|
| Big bold caption / hype | **TheBoldFont** | dafont (free for the regular weight) |
| Bold caption all-purpose | **Montserrat** (ExtraBold/Black) | Google Fonts |
| Rounded bold caption | **Poppins** (Bold/Black) | Google Fonts |
| Tall condensed hook titles | **Anton** | Google Fonts |
| Classic condensed display | **Bebas Neue** | Google Fonts |
| Clean minimal UI/captions | **Inter** (SemiBold) | Google Fonts |
| Friendly geometric | **Outfit** / **Sora** | Google Fonts |
| Handwritten accent | **Caveat** / **Permanent Marker** | Google Fonts |

> Check the license if it's for paid client work — Google Fonts are all OK for commercial use.
> Some dafont fonts are "free for personal use" only; verify before using on client deliverables.

## Vetted free asset sources (safe, license-clear)

### Templates / MOGRTs
- **Adobe Stock — Free templates** (filter: Free + Premiere Pro) — official, safe.
- **Mixkit** — free Premiere templates, no attribution, commercial OK.
- **Motion Array** — free tier (account required).

### Stock video / overlays
- **Pexels**, **Pixabay** — free, commercial use.
- **Mixkit** — free clips & overlays.

### Sound FX & music
- **Pixabay**, **Mixkit**, **Uppbeat**, **YouTube Audio Library**, **Freesound** (check CC0).

### Icons / graphics
- **Flaticon**, **The Noun Project** (attribution on free tier), **Figma community** files.

---

## ⚠️ Avoid these (malware risk)

The "free download" mirror sites that repackage **paid** packs (e.g. results like *psdly,
xtemplates, gfxplugin, intro-hd, freevideoeffect, gfxlife*) are pirated files and a common
malware vector — cracked motion-graphics files are a classic delivery method for trojans.
Don't download the Sparvol/MotionText/etc. packs from these. Build your own with this kit, or
buy direct if you want the real thing.

---

## If you'd rather buy (cheap & legit)

If your time is worth more than the build:
- **Envato Elements** (~$16.50/mo) or **Motion Array** (~$19.99/mo) — unlimited MOGRT/caption
  packs; one month beats a single $77 pack.
- **Gumroad / Etsy / Creative Market** — indie Premiere caption packs, often **$5–15**.
- Always confirm a pack is **Premiere `.mogrt`** (not DaVinci-only) before buying.
